# Common Interview Questions

### Is ChatGPT Just an LLM?

No.

It is a complete AI product composed of multiple systems.

---

### Why Does Perplexity Need Search?

LLMs alone cannot reliably provide current information.

---

### Why Does Cursor Feel Better Than ChatGPT for Coding?

Because Cursor has access to the codebase context.

---

### Why Are Memory Systems Important?

They allow personalization and continuity.

---

### Why Is Conversation Management Necessary?

Context windows are limited.
