# Common Interview Questions

### Why Are GPUs Used for LLMs?

Because they excel at parallel matrix computations.

---

### What is Quantization?

Reducing numerical precision to lower memory and computation requirements.

---

### What is Batching?

Processing multiple requests together for better efficiency.

---

### Why Use Load Balancers?

To distribute traffic across servers.

---

### What is Sharding?

Splitting data across multiple systems.

---

### Why Is Caching Important?

It reduces latency and infrastructure cost.

---

### What is Multi-Tenancy?

Serving multiple customers on a shared platform while isolating their data.
