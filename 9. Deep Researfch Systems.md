# Common Interview Questions

### What is Deep Research?

A multi-step AI workflow that performs planning, retrieval, reasoning, validation, and report generation.

---

### Why is Planning Important?

It decomposes complex research goals into manageable tasks.

---

### What is Iterative Retrieval?

Repeated searching based on newly discovered information.

---

### Why Use Reflection Loops?

To identify missing information and improve completeness.

---

### Why Are Citations Important?

They provide traceability and verification.

---

### What is Long-Horizon Reasoning?

Reasoning across many steps instead of a single LLM call.
