# Why This Module Matters

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make:

```text
Application Works
=
Application Is Good
```

Not true.

---

Traditional software is easy to test.

Example:

```python
2 + 2 = 4
```

Expected answer is known.

---

AI systems are different.

Question:

```text
Explain LangGraph.
```

Possible good answers:

```text
Answer A
Answer B
Answer C
```

All may be correct.

---

This creates a new challenge:

```text
How do we measure quality?
```

---

# The Production Reality

Building AI is only:

```text
30%
```

of the work.

---

Evaluating and improving it is often:

```text
70%
```

of the work.

---

Companies spend huge effort on:

* Evaluation
* Testing
* Monitoring
* Benchmarking

because:

```text
Bad Evaluation
=
Bad Product
```

---

## Traditional Software

```text

 ↓

 ↓
Expected Output
```

---

Example

```python

```

---

## AI Systems

```text
Input
 ↓

 ↓
Probabilistic Output
```

---

Multiple valid answers exist.

---

Example

Question:

```text

```

---

Good Answer 1:

```text
Retrieval-Augmented Generation...
```

---

Good Answer 2:

```text
A technique that combines retrieval...
```

---

Both are correct.

# 1. LLM Evaluation Fundamentals

Let's start with the model output.

---

# Question

How good is this answer?

---

We typically measure:

---

## Correctness

Is the answer accurate?

---

Example

Question:

```text
Capital of France?
```

Answer:

```text
Paris
```

Correct.

---

# Relevance

Does the answer address the question?

---

Question:

```text
Explain RAG.
```

Answer:

```text
RAG explanation
```

Relevant.

---

Answer:

```text
Python tutorial
```

Not relevant.

---

# Completeness

Did the answer cover everything?

---

Question:

```text
Explain Agentic RAG.
```

---

Answer:

```text
Only partial explanation.
```

May be correct but incomplete.

---

# Clarity

Can users understand it?

---

Bad

```text
Highly complex explanation.
```

---

Good

```text
Simple explanation.
```

---

# Human Analogy

Teacher grading an essay.

---

Evaluates:

```text
Accuracy
Coverage
Clarity
```

---

# 2. RAG Evaluation

One of the most important topics.

---

# Common Misconception

Developers evaluate:

```text
Final Answer
```

only.

---

Wrong.

---

Must evaluate:

```text
Retrieval
+
Generation
```

---

# RAG Pipeline

```text
Question
 ↓
Retriever
 ↓
Context
 ↓
LLM
 ↓
Answer
```

---

Both stages matter.

---

# Retrieval Evaluation

Ask:

```text
Did we retrieve the right information?
```

---

# Metric 1: Recall

Did retrieval find relevant documents?

---

Example

Relevant Docs:

```text
A
B
C
```

---

Retrieved:

```text
A
B
```

Recall is lower because C was missed.

---

# Metric 2: Precision

Were retrieved documents useful?

---

Example

Retrieved:

```text
A
B
X
Y
```

---

Only:

```text
A
B
```

are relevant.

---

Precision drops.

---

# Metric 3: Context Relevance

Does retrieved context actually help answer the question?

---

Example

Question:

```text
What is PTO?
```

---

Retrieved:

```text
Vacation Policy
```

Useful.

---

Retrieved:

```text
Company Parking Rules
```

Not useful.

---

# Generation Evaluation

Evaluate:

```text
Faithfulness
```

---

Question:

Did answer stay grounded in retrieved documents?

---

# Example

Document:

```text
Employees receive 20 leave days.
```

---

Answer:

```text
Employees receive 25 leave days.
```

Hallucination.

---

Faithfulness is poor.

---

# Golden Rule

```text
Bad Retrieval
=
Bad Generation
```

---

---

Example

```text

```

---

```text

```

---

```text

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓
Score
```

---

```text

Correctness
Completeness
Relevance
```

---

```text

```

---

# Benefits

✅ Cheap

✅ Scalable

# Limitations


# 9. Regression Testing

A critical production practice.

---

# Problem

You improve a prompt.

---

Unexpectedly:

```text
Other Tasks Become Worse
```

---

Example

Version 1:

```text
Accuracy: 90%
```

---

Version 2:

```text
Accuracy: 82%
```

---

Regression occurred.

---

# Solution

Run tests before deployment.

---

Example Suite

```text
Question 1
Question 2
Question 3
...
Question 100
```

---

Every release must pass.

---

# Why Important?

Prevents quality degradation.

---

# Common Interview Questions

### Why is AI Testing Different?

Because outputs are probabilistic rather than deterministic.

---

### What is Faithfulness?

Whether an answer stays grounded in provided evidence.

---

### Precision vs Recall?

Precision:

```text
How much retrieved content was relevant?
```

Recall:

```text
How much relevant content was retrieved?
```

---

### What is LLM-as-a-Judge?

Using an LLM to evaluate another model's output.

---

### Why is Tracing Important?

It helps debug multi-step AI workflows.
