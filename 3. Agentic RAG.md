# The Problem with Traditional RAG

Traditional RAG is passive.

---

Example:

Question:

```text
Who founded OpenAI and what companies did they previously work at?
```

Traditional RAG:

```text
Single Search
      ↓
Retrieve Documents
      ↓
Generate Answer
```

---

But what if:

* One search isn't enough?
* Information exists across multiple sources?
* Retrieved documents are incomplete?
* Additional searches are needed?

Traditional RAG struggles.

---

# Example Failure

Question:

```text
How did the technology developed by Company A influence Product B?
```

Answer may require:

```text
Document 1
+
Document 2
+
Document 3
```

Traditional RAG often misses connections.

---

# Enter Agentic RAG

Instead of:

```text
Search Once
```

Agentic RAG:

```text
Plan
 ↓
Search
 ↓
Evaluate
 ↓
Search Again
 ↓
Combine Evidence
 ↓
Answer
```

---

# Human Analogy

Imagine writing a research paper.

You don't:

```text
Read One Page
 ↓
Write Paper
```

---

You:

```text
Search Sources
 ↓
Read
 ↓
Take Notes
 ↓
Find Missing Information
 ↓
Search Again
 ↓
Write Paper
```

That is Agentic RAG.

The first major upgrade.

---

# Traditional RAG

```text

 ↓

 ↓

```

---

# Agentic RAG

```text
Question
 ↓
Plan Retrieval Strategy
 ↓
Execute Searches
 ↓
Combine Results
```

```text
Compare LangChain and LangGraph adoption trends.
```

Then combines evidence.

# Why Planning Matters

Complex questions often require:

```text
Multiple Retrieval Steps
```

instead of one.

# 2. Query Decomposition

One of the most powerful techniques.

---

# Problem

Complex questions contain multiple sub-questions.

---

Example

```text
Who founded OpenAI and what companies did they work at before OpenAI?
```

---

Actually contains:

```text
Question 1:
Who founded OpenAI?

Question 2:
Where did they work before?
```

---

# Query Decomposition

Agent splits:

```text
Large Question
```

into:

```text
Small Questions
```

---

Flow

```text
Question
 ↓
Decompose
 ↓

Sub Question A
Sub Question B
Sub Question C

 ↓

Combine Answers
```

---

# Benefits

✅ Better retrieval

✅ Better reasoning

✅ More accurate answers

---

# Human Analogy

Breaking a large project into tasks.

---

# 3. Multi-Hop Retrieval

Very common in research systems.

---

# What is Multi-Hop?

Answer requires multiple retrieval steps.

---

Example

Question:

```text
Who is the CEO of the company that acquired GitHub?
```

---

Step 1

```text
Who acquired GitHub?
```

Answer:

```text
Microsoft
```

---

Step 2

```text
Who is Microsoft's CEO?
```

Answer:

```text
Satya Nadella
```

---

Traditional RAG often fails.

---

Agentic RAG performs:

```text
Retrieve
 ↓
Reason
 ↓
Retrieve Again
 ↓
Answer
```

---

# Multi-Hop Architecture

```text
Question
 ↓

Search
 ↓

Intermediate Fact
 ↓

Search Again
 ↓

Answer
```

---

# 4. Self-Correcting RAG

A powerful production technique.

---

# Problem

Retrieval fails.

---

Traditional RAG:

```text
Bad Retrieval
 ↓
Bad Answer
```

---

Agentic RAG:

```text
Bad Retrieval
 ↓
Detect Failure
 ↓
Search Again
 ↓
Better Answer
```

---

# Example

Agent asks:

```text
Did retrieval provide enough evidence?
```

---

If:

```text
No
```

---

Perform another search.

---

# Human Analogy

Researching a topic.

You realize:

```text
Not Enough Information
```

and continue researching.

---

# 5. Reflection-Based Retrieval

Agents evaluate their own retrieval quality.

---

# Example

Agent retrieves:

```text
Document A
Document B
```

---

Then asks:

```text
Do these documents answer the question?
```

---

If:

```text
No
```

---

Search again.

---

Flow

```text
Retrieve
 ↓
Evaluate
 ↓

Good?
 ↓

Yes → Continue

No → Retrieve Again
```

---

# Benefits

✅ Better evidence quality

✅ Fewer hallucinations

---

---


---

```text
One Search
```

---

Complex Question

```text
Compare LangGraph, CrewAI and AutoGen
for enterprise AI systems.
```

---

Requires:

```text
Multiple Searches
```

---

# Adaptive Retrieval

Agent decides:

```text
Easy Question
 ↓
Simple Retrieval

Hard Question
 ↓
Advanced Retrieval
```

---

Benefits

✅ Lower cost

✅ Faster responses

✅ Better scalability

# Common Interview Questions

### Why does traditional RAG fail?

Because it usually performs a single retrieval step and cannot adapt.

---

### What is Agentic RAG?

A retrieval system that uses agent-like reasoning, planning, and iterative retrieval.

---

### What is Multi-Hop Retrieval?

Retrieving information through multiple reasoning steps.

---

### What is Query Decomposition?

Breaking complex questions into smaller retrieval tasks.

---

### What is Reflection-Based Retrieval?

Evaluating retrieval quality and re-searching if evidence is insufficient.
